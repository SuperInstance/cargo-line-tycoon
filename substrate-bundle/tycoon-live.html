🎯 How to Play

1. Pick a Locale

Each locale teaches the substrate through its own pedagogical lens. Choose the one that resonates with you. The same cell-graph, different framing.

2. Plan a Route

Pick a port, plan a route through the cell-graph. Each waypoint is a cell. Your route gets hash-chained into the witness-log.

3. Trade & Negotiate

Engage with AI agents (3 per locale). They ask questions, validate your route, witness your cargo. They become guides as you learn.

4. Submit to Canons

Successful routes get added to the canon. The substrate learns from your play. Playtesters are guides; their patterns become pedagogy.

🌐 Distributed Computing

While you play, your computer contributes to the Quilt. The same browser that runs the game can train models, run PDE solvers, host witness-log replication.
